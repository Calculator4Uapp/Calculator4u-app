# Calculator4U

**185+ free, ad-free, and privacy-first online calculators**

👉 **Live Website:** [https://calculator4u.app](https://calculator4u.app)

Calculator4U is a lightweight collection of specialized calculators covering Finance, Health & Fitness, Environment & Energy, Math, and Pet Health.  
Everything works instantly in your browser — no signup, no ads, and no data tracking.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **185+ specialized calculators**
- 100% free and open access
- Completely ad-free
- Privacy-first (client-side calculations)
- No registration or login required
- Fast and mobile-friendly
- Clean, modern interface

Calculator4U was built to solve a simple problem: most online calculators are cluttered with ads, force signups, or track users.

This project aims to provide clean, accurate, and private tools that just work.
